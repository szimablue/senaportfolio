Documentary: Many Faces of Hisarüstü

Synopsis: Hisar, is it a neighborhood, a memory, or a living character? This documentary tells the story of this historic Bosphorus district through the eyes of five different people. As the camera follows the rhythms of their daily lives, each is asked a single question: “If Hisar were a person, what would it look like?” Their answers transform from words into drawings and the resulting portrait is a collage not only of buildings, streets, and the sea, but also of belonging, change, and personal connection. “Hisar” comes to life on screen as both a place and the shared imagination of those who call it home. 2025
